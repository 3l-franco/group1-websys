What you can do

📄

Upload credentials

New students and transferees upload Form 138, PSA birth certificate and other documents, then track approval status.

📅

Check schedules

See available subjects and sections. Schedule conflicts and full sections are flagged before you enroll.

🔒

Prerequisite check

Subjects with a failed prerequisite are locked automatically, and your allowed unit load is adjusted.

🔔

Payment queue

Get an online queue number and an email or in-app alert when your turn is near.

How enrollment works

  1. Create an account and log in.
  2. New students: upload credentials and wait for staff approval.
  3. Select subjects and sections.
  4. Staff review and approve your enrollment.
  5. Get your queue number and pay at the cashier when called.
