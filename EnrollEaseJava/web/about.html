About EnrollEase

The online enrollment system of the Registrar and Enrollment Services Office, Technological Institute of the Philippines.

Our mission

EnrollEase replaces long enrollment lines with one online system for the Registrar and Enrollment Services Office. Students upload credentials, choose subjects without schedule conflicts, and receive a payment queue number.

Payments are still made at the cashier.

Our vision

To make enrollment at the Technological Institute of the Philippines fully online, fair and stress-free, so that every student can enroll from anywhere without long lines, schedule errors, or guessing when it is their turn to pay.

We aim for a registrar’s office where students and staff spend their time on learning and service, not on paperwork and waiting.

About the school

Technological Institute of the Philippines (T.I.P.)

The Technological Institute of the Philippines is a private, non-sectarian school of higher education. It was established on February 8, 1962 by a group of educators headed by Engr. Demetrio A. Quirino Jr. and Dr. Teresita U. Quirino, with the goal of providing quality technological education that is within reach of every Filipino.

T.I.P. is known for its programs in engineering, architecture, information technology, maritime studies, business education, and teacher education. It serves students from senior high school up to graduate level.

EnrollEase supports the school’s Registrar and Enrollment Services Office by moving its enrollment steps – credential checking, subject selection, and cashier queuing – online.

Quick facts

  • Founded: February 8, 1962
  • Type: Private, non-sectarian
  • Campuses: Manila (Quiapo) and Quezon City (Cubao)
  • Quezon City campus: 938 Aurora Boulevard, Cubao
  • Program areas: Engineering, Architecture, IT, Maritime, Business, Teacher Education
  • Website: www.tip.edu.ph
